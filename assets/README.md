## Size matters

GitHub Pages soft-caps a published site at **1 GB** and suggests staying under
**100 GB of bandwidth per month**. You will not come close. But every visitor
downloads your images, so resize before committing — a 6 MB camera JPEG makes
the page feel broken on a phone. Aim for under 300 KB per image.

Git also keeps every version of every binary you commit, forever. If you swap
your headshot ten times, all ten live in the repo's history. Not a real problem
at this scale, just worth knowing before you commit a folder of RAW files.
